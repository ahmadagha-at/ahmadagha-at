### 🚀 About Me

I’m a Computer Science student at Johannes Kepler University Linz (JKU), exploring different areas of computer science and software development. I enjoy trying out new ideas through hands-on projects and discovering how things work. So far, I’ve explored backend and fullstack development, and I’m also curious about embedded systems, low-level programming, and AI engineering.

Through my coursework and personal projects, I’m learning and experimenting with Java, Python, C, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Docker, and REST APIs. I’m also exploring how AI can be used in software applications. I’m still figuring out which areas I enjoy most, and I’m excited to keep learning, building, and trying new things.
